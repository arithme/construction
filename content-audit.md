# Content audit — required before real client launch
- [ ] Phone and WhatsApp ownership verified; test call and chat.
- [ ] Email inbox and actual lead delivery tested.
- [ ] Address, service area and business hours confirmed.
- [ ] Credentials checked against source documents and approved for display.
- [ ] Project descriptions, dates, client disclosure and photographs approved.
- [ ] Real testimonials have permission; no fabricated ratings or aggregate review schema.
- [ ] Statistics have an evidence source and date.
- [ ] Sample badges remain wherever sample content remains.
- [ ] No placeholder copy or unsupported pricing claims.
- [ ] Location pages have distinct useful content.
- [ ] Privacy/terms, retention, analytics consent and data processors approved.
- [ ] Production origin, canonical URLs, sitemap and robots verified.
- [ ] Contact pipeline, spam controls and upload scanning verified.
- [ ] Mobile, keyboard, axe, Lighthouse and broken-link gates pass.
